1. Who this applies to

This policy explains what personal data Cornerstone collects when you use our website or mobile application, why we collect it, who we share it with, and what you can ask us to do with it.

The data controller is [[LEGAL ENTITY NAME]], [[REGISTERED ADDRESS]]. Contact details for privacy questions are at the end of this policy.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details — name, mobile number, email address, and the role you use the app in;
  • Delivery details — site and delivery addresses, landmarks, and a contact number for the delivery;
  • Order and booking details — what you ordered, what you booked, and the project or site it belongs to;
  • Business details, for business accounts — business name, GSTIN and billing address;
  • Identity and verification documents, for workers, riders and store staff, where required to work on the platform;
  • Content you submit — site diary entries, photographs, ratings, support messages and complaints.

Information collected as you use the app

  • Location — the delivery address you set, and, for riders on an active delivery, position reported while the delivery is in progress. For workers, a check-in and check-out location at the site boundary for attendance;
  • Device and usage data — device model, operating system, app version, language, IP address, crash reports and diagnostic logs;
  • Transaction records — order history, invoices, payment status and refund records.

We do not collect or store your payment credentials. Full card numbers, CVV, UPI PIN and net-banking credentials are entered with our payment gateway partner and never reach our systems. We receive only the result of the transaction and a non-sensitive reference for it.

4. Who we share it with

We share personal data only where it is needed, and only as follows:

  • Delivery riders — the delivery address, contact number and order contents needed to complete your delivery;
  • Store teams — order details needed to pick and pack;
  • Payment gateway — the details needed to process a payment or refund;
  • Service providers — hosting, communications, mapping, analytics and customer-support tools, bound to use the data only for the service they provide to us;
  • Professional advisers — accountants and lawyers, where necessary;
  • Authorities — where we are required by law, a court order or a valid request from a law-enforcement agency;
  • A successor — if the business is sold or reorganised, subject to this policy.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

5. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected for, and for as long as the law requires. Order, invoice and tax records are kept for the statutory retention period applicable in India. Diagnostic logs are kept for a short period and then deleted. Rider position recorded for an active delivery is retained only as long as needed to complete and evidence that delivery.

Data no longer needed is deleted or anonymised.

6. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the risk — encrypted transport (HTTPS/TLS), access control, restricted internal access on a need-to-know basis, and secure storage of authentication tokens on your device. Payment credentials are handled entirely by our PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway partner.

No system is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal data we will act on it and notify you and the relevant authority where the law requires.

7. Your rights

Subject to applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • Access a summary of the personal data we hold about you;
  • Correct data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • Erase data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep;
  • Withdraw consent where we relied on it — this does not affect anything done before you withdrew it;
  • Nominate another person to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity;
  • Complain to us, and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

To exercise any of these, write to us using the details below. We respond within 30 days. We may need to verify your identity first, and we may be unable to delete records we are legally required to retain.

8. Permissions the app asks for

  • Location — to set a delivery address accurately, to report a rider's position during an active delivery, and to record a worker's check-in at a site boundary. You can refuse or withdraw this in your device settings; some features will not work without it;
  • Camera and photos — to add photographs to a site diary, a return request or a verification document;
  • Notifications — to tell you about the status of an order or booking.

9. Cookies

This website uses only what is necessary to serve the pages. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies on it. Where we add analytics, this policy will be updated first and any consent required will be asked for.

10. Children

Cornerstone is not intended for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The version in force is the one published here, with the date shown at the top. Material changes will be notified in the app or by email.
