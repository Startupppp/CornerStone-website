What to contact us about

Use the email or phone above for anything to do with an order, a delivery, a labour booking, a refund, an invoice, or a complaint. We aim to acknowledge every message within 24 hours and to resolve routine issues within 3 working days. Anything that needs a site visit or a supplier check may take longer, and we will tell you so rather than leave the ticket silent.

For faster help with a specific order, quote your order number in the first message.
