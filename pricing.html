Pricing

What things cost, and how.

Last updated 16 August 2026

Currency and taxes

All prices on Cornerstone are in Indian Rupees (₹) and apply to delivery within our served Hyderabad zones only. Prices shown are inclusive of GST where GST applies, at the rate in force for that category of goods. The tax component is itemised on the order summary and on the invoice issued for the order.

There are no hidden charges. The amount shown on the checkout screen — goods, delivery fee and taxes — is the amount payable. Nothing is added after you confirm.

Materials

Materials are priced per unit and sold in the units the trade actually uses — bags, pieces, tonnes, cubic metres, litres, running metres. Every product shows its unit, its price for that unit, and any minimum order quantity before you add it to the cart.

Prices move with the market. Cement, steel and aggregate in particular are repriced as our own purchase cost changes. The price that applies to your order is the price shown at the moment you place it — a later change does not affect an order already confirmed.

Cornerstone owns the stock it sells. You are buying from us at a listed price, not bidding across third-party shops, and the price you see is the price that ships.

Delivery fees

Charged on order value before taxes, and shown at checkout before payment:

There is no minimum order value. Where an order is split across more than one vehicle because it mixes heavy and light goods, the delivery fee is charged once. Full terms are in the Shipping and Delivery Policy.

Labour

Workers are booked by the day or by the gang, at a rate shown before you confirm the booking. The rate depends on the trade, the skill level and the duration. The booking screen shows the total payable for the period booked, and any Cornerstone service fee is itemised separately rather than buried in the rate.

Overtime, material and transport are not included in a labour rate unless the booking says so.

How you pay

Payment is collected against the order at checkout or on delivery, depending on the method you choose. Online payments are processed by our payment gateway partner — Cornerstone does not receive or store your full card number, CVV, UPI PIN or net-banking credentials.

MethodWhen charged

Available methods are shown at checkout and may vary by order value, delivery zone and item type. Cornerstone does not operate an escrow service and does not hold funds in trust between parties.

Refunds are made to the original payment method under the Cancellation and Refunds Policy.

Estimates

The in-app estimator converts a measurement and a specification into quantities and an indicative cost at current catalogue rates. An estimate is a guide, not a quotation. It does not reserve stock or fix a price, and the amount payable is always the total shown at checkout.

Questions about a charge

If a charge on an order does not look right, contact us using the details on the Contact Us page with the order number. We will explain the line or correct it.
