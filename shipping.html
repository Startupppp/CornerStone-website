Shipping and Delivery Policy.

Last updated 16 August 2026

Where we deliver

Cornerstone delivers construction materials from its own stores to four zones in Hyderabad, Telangana. We do not deliver outside these zones. Serviceability is checked against the delivery pincode before an order can be placed — if your site is outside a served zone, the order is not accepted.

Delivery timelines

Orders are placed and delivered between 08:00 and 20:00 IST, every day. Three delivery modes are offered:

ModeTimelineNotes
ASAP60 – 120 minutesFrom order confirmation, within operating hours
Scheduled slot2-hour windowChosen by you at checkout
Next dayNext operating daySlot confirmed at checkout

Every order carries a promised delivery window recorded at the time it is placed. You can see that window, and the progress of the order against it, in the app.

Delivery windows are estimates, not guarantees. Traffic, weather, restricted site access, local restrictions and demand spikes can push a delivery past its window. Where a delivery is running late we will tell you rather than let the window pass silently.

Orders placed after 20:00 IST, or outside operating hours, are scheduled for the next operating day. The 60–120 minute window applies only to ASAP orders placed within operating hours in a served zone.

Delivery charges

Delivery charges are calculated on the order value before taxes and are shown at checkout before you pay.

Order valueDelivery fee
₹10,000 and aboveFree
₹5,000 – ₹9,999₹99
Under ₹5,000₹199

There is no minimum order value. Some items carry a minimum order quantity set by the unit they are sold in — that minimum is shown on the product itself.

Heavy and bulk items

Cement, sand, aggregate, blocks and steel are heavy goods. They are dispatched on vehicles rated for the load, which can mean a different vehicle and a different arrival time from the rest of an order. Where an order mixes heavy and light goods, it may arrive in more than one delivery at no extra charge.

Delivery is to the site access point. Our riders will not carry material up floors, across unfinished ground, or into a structure. Where a site needs unloading labour, that must be arranged separately — including through a Cornerstone labour booking.

Site access and receiving the order

Someone must be present at the delivery address to receive and check the order. Please make sure the address is reachable by road, that the vehicle can be parked, and that the contact number on the order is answering.

Check the delivery before the rider leaves. Damage, shortfall or a wrong item is far easier to resolve when it is raised at the doorstep — see the Cancellation and Refunds Policy.

If we cannot deliver

If nobody is available, the site is unreachable, or the contact number does not answer, the rider will wait a reasonable period and then return the order to the store. We will contact you to arrange redelivery. A redelivery attempt may be charged at the applicable delivery fee, and repeated failed attempts may result in the order being cancelled and refunded under the Cancellation and Refunds Policy.

Tracking

Once an order is dispatched you can follow it in the app. Tracking shows the order's status, the promised window, and the rider's last reported position — it is not a continuous, second-by-second live trace, and we do not describe it as one.

Questions

For anything about a delivery, contact us using the details on the Contact Us page, quoting your order number.
