The problem / why this exists

A build in Hyderabad still runs on phone calls.

The cement arrives a day late. The mason who confirmed on Tuesday sends someone else on Wednesday. Nobody agrees on what was delivered, what was paid, or who was on site — because none of it was ever written down.

Every part of that is a coordination problem, and coordination problems are software. Cornerstone puts the catalogue, the crew, the delivery and the money record in one place, so the site owner can answer what arrived, who worked, what it cost without making a single call.

What we're building / the product

Two things nobody has put in one app.

Materials quick-commerce that actually understands construction units and heavy loads — and the labour, attendance and project tooling that a materials app has no idea about.

Dark stores

We own the stock

No shopkeeper roulette. Branded cement, TMT steel, sand, blocks, plumbing and electrical sit in our own stores, one per zone — so the price you see is the price that ships.

SLA clock

60–120 minutes, on a timer

Every order carries a promised window from the moment it is placed. The clock is visible to you, to the store picking it and to the ops desk watching for it to slip.

Labour

Crew on demand

Masons, electricians, plumbers, painters and helpers — booked by day or by gang, tracked from request to completion.

Attendance

Geofenced check-in

Workers check in at the site boundary, not from a tea stall two kilometres away. Hours become a record, not an argument.

Projects

Sites, not just carts

Group orders, bookings, photos and diary entries under a site. Six months later you can still see what happened in week three.

Fleet

Our riders, our route

Dispatch from the nearest in-zone store, with the rider's last reported location on the order — and heavy-item rules that stop a 50 kg bag going out on a two-wheeler.

Warehouse OS

Pick, pack, count

Pick lists off the order lines, stock movements on every write, cycle counts that refuse to close if stock would go negative.

Money

A ledger, not a number

Balances move as double-entry rows in one transaction, or they don't move at all. Every amount on your screen can be traced to the line that created it.

Estimator

Slab to shopping list

Enter the area and the mix; get quantities in the units the trade actually uses — bags, tonnes, cubic metres, pieces — priced against live catalogue rates.

Order flow / pincode to doorstep

How an order actually runs.

Step 01

Pincode check

We tell you straight away whether your site is inside a serviced zone. If it isn't, we say so instead of taking the order.

Step 02

Cart, no minimum

One bag or one truckload. No minimum order value beyond the SKU's own minimum quantity.

Step 03

Pick your window

ASAP in 60–120 minutes, a scheduled two-hour slot, or next day.

Open 08:00–20:00 IST, every day
Step 04

Nearest store picks it

The order is assigned to the closest in-zone dark store that actually holds the stock, and turns into a pick list on the floor.

Free cancel within 10 minutes
Step 05

Dispatch and clock

A rider takes it, the SLA clock runs against the promised window, and the order carries the rider's last reported position.

Step 06

Delivered — and reversible

Unused goods in original packing can be returned or exchanged within 24 hours. Cut and mixed goods can't, and we tell you which is which before you buy.

Straight answers / before you ask

What we promise, and what we don't.

Construction is full of people promising things they can't deliver. We would rather lose the sign-up than earn it on a claim we can't keep.

What we will do

  • 60–120 minutes in the four listed zones, during opening hours.
  • Our stock, our stores. Branded goods we hold ourselves.
  • A promised window on every order, and a clock you can watch against it.
  • 24-hour returns on unused goods in original packing.
  • Free cancellation for 10 minutes after you place the order.
  • Every rupee on a ledger line you can trace.

What we will not claim

  • Not 10-minute delivery. Cement is not groceries.
  • Not all of Hyderabad. Four zones at launch, and we'll name them every time.
  • Not second-by-second tracking. You see the rider's last reported position, not a live trace.
  • Not escrow. Payment is settled on delivery at launch; anything else would be a story.
  • Not a marketplace. You are not bidding across shops — you are buying from us.
