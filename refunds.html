Cancellation and Refunds Policy.

Last updated 16 August 2026

Cancelling an order

You can cancel an order free of charge within 10 minutes of placing it, provided the store has not already started picking it. Cancel from the order screen in the app.

After picking has started, or after the 10-minute window has passed, cancellation is not automatic. Contact us and we will cancel where the order has not yet been dispatched. Once an order is out for delivery it cannot be cancelled — you may refuse it at the door instead, and it will be treated as a return under this policy.

We may cancel an order if the goods turn out to be unavailable, the delivery address is not serviceable, the address cannot be reached, or we reasonably believe the order is fraudulent. Where we cancel an order you have already paid for, the full amount including any delivery fee is refunded.

Returns

You may request a return within 24 hours of delivery. To be accepted, goods must be:

  • Unused and unopened, in their original packaging;
  • In resaleable condition, with any seals, tags and labels intact;
  • Accompanied by the order number.

Once a return is approved, we collect the goods from the delivery address. Approved returns are inspected at the store before a refund is raised.

Goods that cannot be returned

Construction materials are often altered or committed the moment they are used. The following are not returnable, and this is stated on the product before you buy:

  • Cut-to-size goods — steel, pipe, board or any item cut to your measurement;
  • Mixed or tinted paint, and any custom-mixed product;
  • Opened cement, adhesive or chemical bags, and any bag whose seal is broken;
  • Loose bulk material once tipped — sand, aggregate, soil and similar;
  • Made-to-order and specially procured items;
  • Any item marked non-returnable on its product page.

This does not affect your rights where goods are damaged, defective, short-supplied or not what you ordered.

Damaged, short or wrong items

Check your delivery before the rider leaves. If an item is damaged, missing, or not what you ordered, raise it at the doorstep or contact us within 24 hours of delivery with photographs and the order number.

We will replace the item, complete the short-supplied quantity, or refund it — your choice, and at no cost to you. The exclusions above do not apply to damaged, defective or wrongly supplied goods.

Exchanges

An exchange for a different size, grade or variant may be requested on the same terms and within the same 24-hour window as a return. Any price difference is charged or refunded accordingly.

Refunds

Once a cancellation is confirmed, or a return is received and inspected, the refund is raised to the original payment method. We do not refund to a different account or instrument.

StageTimeline
Refund approved and raised by usWithin 2 working days
Credited to your account5 – 7 working days
Orders paid on deliveryBank transfer, 5 – 7 working days

The time taken for a refund to appear depends on your bank or card issuer once we have raised it. Where an order paid on delivery is refunded, we will collect the bank details needed to make the transfer.

Delivery fees are refunded in full where we cancelled the order, where the goods were damaged, defective, short or wrong, and where you cancelled within the free cancellation window. Delivery fees are not refunded on a change-of-mind return of correctly supplied goods.

Labour bookings

A labour booking can be cancelled free of charge up to 12 hours before the booked start time. Cancellations after that, or a no-show at the site, may be charged to cover the worker's committed time — the amount is shown before you confirm the cancellation.

If a booked worker does not arrive, tell us and we will arrange a replacement or refund the booking in full.

How to raise a request

Raise a cancellation, return, exchange or refund from the order screen in the app, or contact us using the details on the Contact Us page with your order number. Requests are acknowledged within 24 hours.

If you are not satisfied with how a request was handled, escalate it to the Grievance Officer listed on the Contact Us page.
